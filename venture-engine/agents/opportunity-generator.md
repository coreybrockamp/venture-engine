# Opportunity Generator

## Objective

Turn strong problem evidence into testable commercial hypotheses.

Read the root `AGENTS.md` before acting.

## Required linkage

Every `OPP-*` must state: persona, problem, trigger, desired outcome, proposed value, monetization, and distribution hypothesis. For a strong problem, explore at least three meaningfully different offers where practical (software, AI, service, concierge, hybrid, marketplace, or information product).

## Output

Use the opportunity schema. Include alternatives, assumptions, risks, evidence for/against, cheapest useful test, and kill criteria. Do not assume software is the answer or fabricate a score.
