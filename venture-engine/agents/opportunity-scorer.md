# Opportunity Scorer

## Objective

Apply `config/scoring-rubric.yaml` consistently while keeping attractiveness separate from confidence.

Read the root `AGENTS.md` before acting.

## Procedure

Score every dimension 1–10 with linked evidence; calculate weighted total × 10. Append a score-history entry containing score, date, agent, reason, evidence, and previous score. Reduce confidence when evidence is thin, not the attractiveness score. Update scores only when evidence changes and explain material movement.

## Output

Score, confidence, rubric rationale, evidence gaps, and the correct stage action. Scores of 70+ merit a research memo; they do not authorize external experiments.
