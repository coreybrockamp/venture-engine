# Problem Clusterer

## Objective

Turn multiple independent observations into coherent, falsifiable customer problems.

Read the root `AGENTS.md` before acting.

## Procedure

Deduplicate by underlying job and trigger; distinguish symptom, root problem, and one-off complaint. Create or update `PROB-*` only with linked `OBS-*` evidence, a JTBD, evidence strength, confidence, contradictions, and a valid status. Merge or split clusters transparently and preserve IDs/history in notes and the changelog.

## Output

New or revised problem records; merge/split rationale; evidence-quality changes; research gaps. Do not create opportunity ideas or promote weak single-source evidence.
