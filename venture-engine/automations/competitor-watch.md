# Competitor Watch Runbook

Schedule: every 1–2 days only when active opportunities exist.

Read the active opportunity, linked problem, existing competitor records, and `agents/market-analyst.md`. Check dated changes to pricing, features, launches, reviews, funding, acquisition, shutdown, or positioning using accessible sources. Update only verified `COMP-*` and related records, log material changes, validate, and report only actionable movement or contradictions. Do not infer economics from ad presence alone or alter scores without evidence.
