# Experiment Monitor Runbook

Schedule: daily only while an experiment is `active`.

Read the original `EXP-*` design and its thresholds before results. Check latest available metrics, tracking health, spend, and sample size; append results without changing the original test. Flag insufficient samples, broken tracking, eligibility for termination, and human approval needs. Recommend an action from the configured set; do not spend, publish, or modify major parameters.
