# Weekly Investment Committee Runbook

Schedule: weekly, after the final research run of the week.

Read all material changes since the previous report and follow `agents/investment-committee.md`. Create `reports/weekly/YYYY-MM-DD-investment-committee.md` only when evidence or portfolio state changed. Include research volume, top opportunities, score moves, experiments, actions, contradictions, and decisions required. Preserve rejection reasons. Do not authorize external activity.
