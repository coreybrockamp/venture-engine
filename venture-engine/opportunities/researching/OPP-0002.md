# Opportunity - OPP-0002

## One-Sentence Thesis

A non-legal shared workflow may help families clarify caregiving duties and compensation questions before informal arrangements create hidden workload.

## Persona

PER-001 adult child managing an aging parent.

## Problem

PROB-0004 - care capacity and paid/unpaid trade-offs.

## User / Buyer / Beneficiary

User: adult child or sibling group. Buyer: family or parent (**UNKNOWN**). Beneficiary: caregiver and care recipient.

## Trigger

A caregiver requests pay, reduces work, or needs recurring respite.

## Desired Outcome

A shared record of duties, coverage, compensation questions, and professional referrals.

## Solution Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS:** a productized non-legal workflow organizes decisions while routing legal, tax, and benefits issues to qualified professionals.

## Value Proposition

Create a common operating record before informal caregiving becomes unmanageable.

## Current Alternative

Informal negotiation, elder-law consultation, agencies, and notes.

## Existing Market Evidence

One-time workflow with referral partnerships; no legal advice.

**UNKNOWN / NOT YET ESTABLISHED** beyond documented payment inquiries and paid-family-caregiver context.
