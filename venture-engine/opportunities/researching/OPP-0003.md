# Opportunity - OPP-0003

## One-Sentence Thesis

An employer-sponsored eldercare-navigation benefit may reduce work disruption when employees suddenly need to coordinate a parent’s care.

## Persona

PER-001 employed adult child; employer is the prospective buyer.

## Problem

PROB-0004 - care capacity and paid/unpaid trade-offs.

## User / Buyer / Beneficiary

User: employed adult child. Buyer: employer. Beneficiary: employee, parent, and employer.

## Trigger

Caregiving causes missed work or a sudden increase in care needs.

## Desired Outcome

Time-sensitive logistical help without the employee carrying the full service cost.

## Solution Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS:** an employer-sponsored human care-navigation benefit focused on eldercare transitions.

## Value Proposition

Reduce caregiving disruption during high-friction care moments.

## Current Alternative

Wellthy, HR resources, local agencies, leave, or self-directed coordination.

## Existing Market Evidence

OBS-000002 and OBS-000031 link caregiving to work disruption; AARP/NAC and Wellthy validate the benefit category.

## Competitors / Substitutes

COMP-0005 Wellthy.

## Business Model

B2B employer benefit.

## Pricing Hypothesis

**FACT:** Wellthy employer purchase starts at US$450/month per employee. **HYPOTHESIS:** no independent price is proposed before buyer research.

## Distribution Hypothesis

Employer-benefits partnerships match the buyer and workplace trigger.

## Why Now

**FACT:** AARP/NAC reports work disruption among employed caregivers. **UNKNOWN:** a narrower benefit has buyer demand.

## Evidence For

Observed work impact and an existing employer-funded competitor category.

## Evidence Against

No employer interviews; Wellthy may already dominate the buyer relationship.

## Key Assumptions

Employers budget for the pain; employees use the benefit; a focused entrant differentiates; procurement is feasible.

## Biggest Risk

Incumbent employer benefits may block acquisition and differentiation.

## What Must Be True

Benefits leaders see a specific unmet eldercare-transition gap.

## Cheapest Question To Test Later

Do benefits leaders identify an unmet gap beyond broad caregiving benefits?

## Sources

Linked record sources: OBS-000002, OBS-000031; COMP-0005; AARP/NAC 2025.

## Status

Researching; unscored; no strategic decision.
