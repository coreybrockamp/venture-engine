# Opportunity - OPP-0004

## One-Sentence Thesis

A narrow shared tracker may help adult children assign and close care follow-ups without adopting a full medical or family-care platform.

## Persona

PER-001 adult child coordinating an aging parent’s care.

## Problem

PROB-0001 - healthcare information and task coordination.

## User / Buyer / Beneficiary

User: adult child and helpers. Buyer: adult child or family (**UNKNOWN**). Beneficiary: parent and care team.

## Trigger

A referral, medication change, test, or follow-up has no clear owner.

## Desired Outcome

Every open care task has a named owner and shared context.

## Solution Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS:** a lightweight open-loop tracker, not a medical record or advice service.

## Value Proposition

Make unfinished care tasks visible across family members.

## Current Alternative

Spreadsheets, calendars, portals, Medisafe, CareBridger, CaringBridge, and Wellthy.

## Existing Market Evidence

Multiple observations describe multi-provider coordination; OBS-000032 requests an information-sharing app.

## Competitors / Substitutes

COMP-0001 CareBridger; COMP-0002 Medisafe; COMP-0003 CaringBridge; COMP-0005 Wellthy.

## Business Model

Consumer subscription or family-paid workspace.

## Pricing Hypothesis

**FACT:** CareBridger lists future CA$9.99/18.99 tiers; Medisafe lists US$4.99 monthly options. **HYPOTHESIS:** price must fit low-cost substitutes; willingness to pay is untested.

## Distribution Hypothesis

Google Search and caregiver communities match post-appointment follow-up moments.

## Why Now

**UNKNOWN / NOT YET ESTABLISHED** beyond category growth and existing tools.

## Evidence For

Recurring multi-provider coordination and direct information-sharing help-seeking.

## Evidence Against

OBS-000030 reports helpful spreadsheets/password management; free tools and portals may be enough.

## Key Assumptions

Families see open-loop ownership as distinct; they enter tasks; existing tools are insufficient; a family member pays.

## Biggest Risk

Users will not switch from free familiar tools.

## What Must Be True

The narrow workflow is more useful than portals, spreadsheets, and generic calendars.

## Cheapest Question To Test Later

Do caregivers abandon the workflow because existing tools work well enough?
