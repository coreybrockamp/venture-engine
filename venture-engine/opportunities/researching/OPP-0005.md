# Opportunity - OPP-0005

## One-Sentence Thesis

A non-legal administrative-handoff workflow may help adult children inventory urgent parent tasks after the first missed bill, coverage problem, or loss of self-management.

## Persona

PER-001 adult child newly taking over parent administration.

## Problem

PROB-0002 - fragmented parent administration.

## User / Buyer / Beneficiary

User: adult child. Buyer: adult child or parent (**UNKNOWN**). Beneficiary: parent and family.

## Trigger

First major billing error, missed coverage, hospitalization, or scattered account discovery.

## Desired Outcome

A prioritized task inventory, owners, and escalation points.

## Solution Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS:** a productized triage workflow routes legal, financial, and medical questions to qualified professionals rather than handling them.

## Value Proposition

Reduce overlooked urgent tasks during an administrative handoff.

## Current Alternative

Autopay, portals, password managers, spreadsheets, attorneys, Wellthy, and manual reconstruction.

## Existing Market Evidence

Six observations document bills, access, deadlines, and coverage consequences; the direct category is fragmented.

## Competitors / Substitutes

## Business Model

## Evidence Against
