# Opportunity - OPP-0007

## One-Sentence Thesis

An FSM-connected revenue-exception work queue may help an owner or office manager decide which stalled estimate, incomplete invoice, or unpaid job needs personal intervention today.

## Persona

PER-003 — local service-business owner with roughly 5–25 employees.

## Problem

PROB-0006 — quote-to-cash handoff friction.

## Residual Pain Classification

**B. POORLY SOLVED (HYPOTHESIS).** Core FSM systems automate reminders and quote/invoice workflows, but public records show an estimate backlog, uncertainty over bid follow-up, and a mobile-invoice sync defect requiring desktop rework. This does not demonstrate a common failure after competent setup.

## Why Existing FSM Does Not Fully Solve It

**FACT:** Jobber Connect includes automated quote/invoice follow-up; the other reviewed suites cover quotes, invoices, and payments. **HYPOTHESIS:** automation does not decide which exception merits personal escalation or reveal the specific blocker when field data has not become billable.

## User / Buyer / Beneficiary

User: owner or office manager. Buyer: owner/operator (**HYPOTHESIS**). Beneficiary: owner and office team.

## Trigger

A busy estimate period, unanswered high-value estimate, completed job lacking billable data, failed mobile-to-office sync, or overdue invoice.

## Economic Consequence

**HYPOTHESIS:** delayed intervention can slow sales conversion or cash collection and create office rework. Dollar leakage, close-rate impact, and days-to-payment are **UNKNOWN**.

## Desired Outcome

An explainable daily order of revenue exceptions requiring human action, with the reason and next step visible inside the existing workflow.

## Solution Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS:** an integration that combines existing FSM status data into a human-reviewed queue that distinguishes routine automation from estimates, invoices, or handoffs requiring personal intervention.

## Product Classification

ADD-ON / INTEGRATION. It complements existing quote, invoice, and payment functions rather than replacing them.

## Value Proposition

Focus owner and office attention on revenue that is stalled for a specific operational reason rather than sending generic follow-ups.

## Current Alternative

Owner or office staff manually reviews FSM reports, emails/calls customers, reopens invoices on desktop, uses QuickBooks, or relies on incumbent automated reminders.

## Competitors / Substitutes

Jobber (COMP-0007), Housecall Pro (COMP-0008), ServiceTitan (COMP-0009), Workiz (COMP-0010), FieldPulse (COMP-0011), QuickBooks, office-admin labor, and manual phone/email follow-up.

## Existing Market Evidence

**FACT:** OBS-000049 records twelve unsent estimates during a busy period; OBS-000050 records an owner/operator seeking bid-follow-up guidance as backlog declines; OBS-000064 records recurring mobile-invoice desktop rework. **FACT:** OBS-000060 reports Jobber reduced invoice chasing, a direct adequacy signal.

## Business Model

**HYPOTHESIS:** recurring per-business add-on subscription, conditional on demonstrating incremental conversion, collection speed, or avoided rework.

## Pricing Hypothesis

**FACT:** field-service software is already paid for, and Jobber Connect is US$99/month annual-billed with automated follow-up. **UNKNOWN:** separate willingness to pay for prioritization; no price is selected.

## Distribution Hypothesis

**HYPOTHESIS:** FSM integration ecosystems, QuickBooks-adjacent communities, and owner/trade communities may reach buyers when revenue stalls, but their effectiveness is unvalidated.

## Evidence For

- **FACT:** an estimate backlog and customer-retention risk are documented (OBS-000049).
- **FACT:** an owner/operator sought a manual bid-follow-up approach when backlog fell (OBS-000050).
- **FACT:** a paid FSM user reports recurring invoice rework from a mobile sync defect (OBS-000064).

## Evidence Against

- **FACT:** reviewed incumbents already offer estimate, invoice, payment, reminder, and reporting functions.
- **FACT:** one Jobber reviewer reports reduced invoice chasing after adoption (OBS-000060).
- **UNKNOWN:** whether prioritization—not implementation or discipline—is the residual cause of lost revenue.

## Key Assumptions

- Owners can identify revenue exceptions from existing system data.
- A prioritized queue changes intervention behavior beyond generic reminders.
- The economic benefit can be measured without replacing the FSM.
- An integration can access reliable quote, invoice, and payment status.
- Owners will pay in addition to existing workflow software.

## Biggest Commercial Risk

Existing FSM automations and reports may already adequately handle the task, making the incremental ROI too weak for another subscription.

## Differentiation Test

The product must identify and explain a revenue exception that automated follow-up, a standard FSM report, QuickBooks, and office-admin review would not already address adequately.

## What Must Be True

At least one recurrent owner-level decision is missing from incumbent quote-to-cash tooling and its outcome can be improved through an integration.

## Cheapest Question To Test Later

Which estimates or invoices receive personal intervention today, why, and what information is missing from the incumbent report or automation?

## Sources

OBS-000049, OBS-000050, OBS-000060, OBS-000064; COMP-0007 through COMP-0011; PROB-0006 market-analysis memo.

Researching; unscored; no strategic decision; no experiment designed.
