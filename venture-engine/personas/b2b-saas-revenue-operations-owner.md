# Finance or revenue-operations owner at a B2B SaaS company with non-standard contracts

## Persona ID

`PER-011` — stable; never reassign.

## Definition

Finance or revenue-operations owner accountable for close-period commercial-to-financial reconciliation across CRM, CPQ/billing, ERP/accounting, and reporting systems at a B2B SaaS company with non-standard contracts. Excludes generic finance leadership, generic RevOps, one-time implementations, and routine accounting controls without a recurring cross-system workflow.

## Core Context

**HYPOTHESIS:** Contract changes can require the same commercial terms to remain consistent across multiple paid systems and reporting layers.

## Trigger Events

**HYPOTHESIS:** Amendments, expansions, contractions, usage components, credits, custom schedules, renewals, early terminations, and month-end close.

## Candidate Jobs to Be Done

**HYPOTHESIS:** When a non-standard deal changes, preserve and reconcile the commercial and financial meaning across the operational stack without delaying close or creating incorrect downstream reporting.

## Existing Spending Categories

**HYPOTHESIS:** CRM, CPQ, subscription billing, ERP/accounting, revenue recognition, integration, data warehouse/BI, and finance or RevOps labor.
