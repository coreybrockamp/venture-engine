# Project-Financials / Accounting Manager at a Commercial Contractor

## Persona ID

`PER-012` — stable; never reassign.

## Definition

Project-financials or accounting manager at a commercial general contractor or subcontractor responsible for keeping approved change-order financial data aligned across project-management and accounting/ERP systems. Excludes generic construction workers, generic contractor management, one-time system implementation, and standalone accounting or project-management work without the cross-system change-order workflow.

## Core Context

**HYPOTHESIS:** Approved change orders can require consistent values, states, and line-item meaning across project-management, contract, job-cost, billing, and accounting records.

## Trigger Events

**HYPOTHESIS:** Change creation, approval, revision, synchronization, billing, WIP/job-cost review, project close, and correction of a mismatched or locked synchronized record.

## Candidate Jobs to Be Done

**HYPOTHESIS:** When an approved construction change changes financial state, preserve a traceable and aligned record across operational and accounting systems without delayed billing, incorrect job-cost reporting, or avoidable re-entry.

## Existing Spending Categories

**HYPOTHESIS:** Construction project-management systems, ERP/accounting, change-order/financial modules, integration middleware, implementation support, accounting/project-administration labor, reporting, and spreadsheet-based controls.
