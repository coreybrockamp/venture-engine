# Outpatient Prior-Authorization Operations Lead

## Persona ID

`PER-014` — stable; never reassign.

## Definition

Administrative operations owner at an outpatient medical practice responsible for moving prior-authorization work across EHR/ePA systems and payer-specific channels, including documentation assembly, submission, status follow-up, and administrative handoffs. Roles may include a PA specialist, medical assistant, referral/authorization coordinator, practice operations manager, or centralized authorization team lead. Excludes clinical judgment, treatment choice, medical necessity, payer coverage decisions, and general healthcare administration.

## Core Context

**HYPOTHESIS:** Even after competent EHR/ePA adoption, staff may still move already-known administrative information among payer-specific channels and track responses outside a single workflow.

## Trigger Events

**HYPOTHESIS:** A prescribed medication or ordered service requires payer authorization; a request needs documentation, resubmission, status follow-up, or an administrative response.

## Candidate Jobs to Be Done

**HYPOTHESIS:** Keep the non-clinical authorization handoff complete and traceable across practice and payer channels without treating clinical or payer judgment as removable friction.

## Existing Spending Categories

**HYPOTHESIS:** EHR/practice-management systems, ePA tools, clearinghouses, payer portals, dedicated authorization staff, and outsourced authorization services. Category spend does not prove separate willingness to pay.

## Likely Information Sources

**HYPOTHESIS:** PA specialists and medical assistants, practice-manager communities, EHR user forums, association surveys, product reviews, public job descriptions, and attributable operator case studies.
