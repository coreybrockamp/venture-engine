# Simulation 07 — Airtable spreadsheet-database collaboration

**Historical-positive class:** harder early-evidence positive  
**Initial product period:** 2014–2015  
**Cutoff:** 2015-03-26  
**Simulated decision:** `HOLD`  
**Confidence:** medium

## Persona and workflow

**Persona:** nontechnical team owner maintaining structured shared operational records. **Workflow:** edit/share records in a spreadsheet-like interface while needing data relationships, typed fields, and safer collaboration.

## Incumbents, actors, and falsification

Google Sheets, Access, Quickbase, and bespoke databases were alternatives. User and selector are the operating team; buyer/payer is **weakly plausible**. The evidence shows a design/fit gap, but does not establish two post-adoption accounts paying labor to bridge a residual workflow. The same HN context creates concentration risk.
