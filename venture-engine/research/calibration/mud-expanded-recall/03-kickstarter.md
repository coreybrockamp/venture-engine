# 03 — Kickstarter (simulation)

**Cutoff:** 2011-04-28 · **Class:** `MUD_NATIVE` · **Richness:** HIGH · **Official decision:** `AUTHORIZE_SCOUT`

**Reconstruction:** independent creator → finance a bounded creative/product project → conventional funding requires gatekeepers or minimum scale → pitches to audiences and seeks pledges → project remains unfunded or delayed. Creator is user/beneficiary; creator pays platform fees and backers fund rewards. Public pledge aggregation plus payment infrastructure are the enabler.
