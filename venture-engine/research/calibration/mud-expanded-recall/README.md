# Expanded MUD Historical Calibration

This is a retrospective, non-canonical calibration of the frozen Market Unlock Discovery (MUD) design. It does not create a MUD candidate, persona, Observation, Problem, Opportunity, or authority for Scout. Only evidence dated at or before each stated cutoff is used to replay the unchanged nine-condition gate.

The portfolio was frozen in `portfolio-selection.md` before case scoring. `PRE_CUTOFF_DIRECT` and `PRE_CUTOFF_INDIRECT` items may affect an official simulated decision; `POST_CUTOFF_OUTCOME_ONLY` and `UNUSABLE_HINDSIGHT` do not. The cards record evidence limitations as findings, rather than filling gaps with later success narratives.

## Context-independence declaration

Case selection used the approved MUD classes, contemporaneous public-source availability, and calibration value only. It did not use user memory, prior conversations, personal background, projects, or interests.

## Decision vocabulary

`AUTHORIZE_SCOUT`, `HOLD`, and `REJECT` retain their approved MUD definitions. The authorization simulation is historical only; it is not human approval and cannot start Scout. `SUBSTANTIVE_FAIL`, `OBSERVABILITY_FAIL`, and `MIXED` identify why a failed condition did not pass.
