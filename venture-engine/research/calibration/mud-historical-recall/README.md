# MUD Historical Recall Backtest

These are retrospective, non-canonical simulations of the approved nine-condition MUD gate. They reuse the eight fixed cases and cutoffs from the CFD calibration, preserve its evidence labels, and do not create MUD candidates, personas, or canonical records. Only `PRE_CUTOFF_DIRECT` and `PRE_CUTOFF_INDIRECT` evidence controls a decision; later outcomes are firewall-protected.

The backtest evaluates MUD recall and false-positive resistance, not whether any historical business was good in hindsight. It does not change MUD, CFD, controller, batch, Scout, or downstream policy.
