# Control Safety Review

This was a lightweight methodology review, not a control re-research or rescore.

| Control | Original result | Source-strategy risk | Preservation finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Glass | REJECT | Technology/infrastructure sources could be mistaken for demand | The strategy explicitly confines them to Gate 8 and still requires two behavioral chains, buyer/payer, and falsification. |
| Color | REJECT | Community attention or launch coverage could be counted as desire | The strategy requires observed attempt/substitute/consequence, not funding, hype, or aspirational social interest. |
| Juicero | REJECT | Pricing and convenience sources could be mistaken for access exclusion | Mandatory cheap/simple-alternative counter-search makes existing juicers/prepared juice disconfirming evidence. |

**Finding:** no source-method-induced false-positive pathway was identified. Preserve all three original `REJECT` decisions.
