# Step 6 Scout Checkpoint — PER-001

## Scope

20 public, paraphrased Reddit observations were collected before this checkpoint. No market analysis, opportunities, experiments, or external actions were performed.

## Evidence Summary

- Observations: 20; independent source URLs: 8; source type: Reddit (20).
- Evidence strength: 20 observations at level 4 (problem evidence); no Tier 1–3 behavioral proof.
- Strongest themes: healthcare coordination (5), administrative coordination (6), family coordination (3), care capacity/service decisions (3), medication/transition evidence (3).
- Existing-spend signals: paid caregiver coverage and home-care trade-offs appear in observations; amounts and willingness to pay are unknown.
- Urgency: hospitalization, missed bills/deadlines, medication routines, caregiver burnout, and provider-access barriers.

## Quality Review

Workarounds include spreadsheets/workbooks, lists, portals, autopay, and manual family calls. Contradictions: portals, authorization, and autopay may relieve portions of the work; no evidence establishes their adequacy at scale. The batch is Reddit-only, self-selected, and may overrepresent high-conflict or high-severity situations.

## Evidence Gaps

No direct purchase behavior beyond reported care-cost trade-offs; no non-Reddit source type; no frequency/incidence measure; no evidence yet about which families would adopt a shared workflow.
