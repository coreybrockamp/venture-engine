# PER-003 Scout Checkpoint - Local Service-Business Owner

## Scope

**FACT:** This Scout-only pass added atomic observations for owner/operators of local field-service firms with roughly 5-25 employees. It created no Problem, market analysis, Opportunity, score, experiment, or automation. Findings are attributed and paraphrased; review-platform incentives, age, non-owner roles, and partial headcount matches are retained in record notes.

## Strongest commercial pain signals

- **Revenue leakage / sales follow-up:** a contractor reported a backlog of twelve estimates while a customer indicated an unanswered estimate could lead them to move on (OBS-000049); another contractor actively sought a non-pushy bid-follow-up method as work declined (OBS-000050).
- **Paid systems and switching pressure:** target-range operators report field-service software spend for dispatch, AR, job costing, and operations, alongside renewal-fee, onboarding, per-user, and fit concerns (OBS-000048, OBS-000055, OBS-000058, OBS-000062).
- **Recurring admin labor:** manual office rework persists where mobile invoices fail to sync, schedules must be manually ordered, or paper/handwritten information is carried into invoicing (OBS-000057, OBS-000063, OBS-000064).
- **Capacity and operational coordination:** a 15-technician shop outgrew a shared calendar; an eight-route pest-control company uses QuickBooks for CRM, payments, routes, and tickets while evaluating alternatives (OBS-000051, OBS-000053).

## Contradictions and adequacy signals

- **FACT:** Several systems are reported to work well: a seven-staff business used Jobber for job-level profit tracking (OBS-000048), a seven-truck company used Wintac for dispatch and AR (OBS-000055), and a pond-service reviewer credited Jobber with operating above US$1 million without admin staff (OBS-000061).
- **FACT:** Some complaints are adoption, fit, or implementation issues rather than evidence of an unsolved category-wide job (OBS-000045, OBS-000058, OBS-000066).
- **CAUTION:** Review-site evidence includes vendor-referred or incentivized reviews; it establishes paid use and concrete workflow detail, but not unbiased prevalence.

## Evidence gaps

- Direct owner statements from explicitly 5-25 employee firms on lost revenue, margin, and hiring remain limited.
- No verified record quantifies missed-call losses, quote conversion, collections delay, or dispatch utilization for this persona.
- No representative evidence shows which pains persist after competent implementation of existing field-service software.
- The sample is software-heavy; staffing, training, material coordination, and reputation management require more independent owner evidence.

## Researcher bias risks

Software review pages naturally surface tool features and implementation complaints, risking an overemphasis on software solutions. Public forums overrepresent people seeking help and can mix owner, office, employee, and vendor voices. Older forum posts are retained only as historical workflow evidence, not as current market prevalence.

## Candidate themes needing more evidence

Before clustering, seek independent owner-level evidence on: estimate follow-up and conversion; job-costing/margin visibility; payment collection; technician capacity; and whether manual work remains after using Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, or similar tools.
