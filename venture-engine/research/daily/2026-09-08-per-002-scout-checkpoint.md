## Observed themes (not formal Problem clusters)

- **Rehab-to-training handoff and professional coordination (9 observations):** Athletes described rebuilding after PT, translating general return guidance into training, and coordinating clinical/coaching input. The clearest post-adoption case is a runner whose hired coach and physio gave incompatible plans and would not coordinate ([OBS-000160](../../data/observations.jsonl)). Other records show paid coach-supported training can successfully absorb the transition ([OBS-000164](../../data/observations.jsonl), [OBS-000169](../../data/observations.jsonl)).
- **Progression, symptom tracking, and adherence (7):** Athletes want clear progression criteria and between-session tracking. App reviews show both useful structured delivery ([OBS-000172](../../data/observations.jsonl), [OBS-000174](../../data/observations.jsonl)) and narrow post-adoption friction when delayed symptoms or schedule changes cannot be represented ([OBS-000173](../../data/observations.jsonl), [OBS-000175](../../data/observations.jsonl)).
- **Existing paid stacks and access (4):** Reported behavior includes PT, coach-supported plans, paid running subscriptions/plans, insurance-provided digital care, remote training, sports massage/recovery, and exercise-app subscriptions. These establish category use or spend—not willingness to pay for a new coordination layer.

## Paid behavior and buyer/payer evidence

**FACT:** Athlete accounts report paid coaching/training after PT ([OBS-000164](../../data/observations.jsonl), [OBS-000169](../../data/observations.jsonl)); purchased plan or paid app use ([OBS-000166](../../data/observations.jsonl)); a high PT copay that changed care behavior ([OBS-000163](../../data/observations.jsonl)); insurance-provided digital care ([OBS-000167](../../data/observations.jsonl), [OBS-000176](../../data/observations.jsonl)); and paid recovery/physio services ([OBS-000178](../../data/observations.jsonl), [OBS-000179](../../data/observations.jsonl)).

**UNKNOWN:** The records rarely identify the selector, direct payer, price, or whether an athlete would buy an additional tool after PT, coaching, app, or insurance coverage. Health/fitness category spend is not residual-job willingness to pay.

## Post-adoption failure and incumbent adequacy

**Post-adoption workflow evidence:**

- A coach and physio supplied incompatible return-to-running plans and did not coordinate ([OBS-000160](../../data/observations.jsonl)).
- A runner reported no structured return plan after PT ([OBS-000162](../../data/observations.jsonl)); the record does not establish care quality.
- Exakt reviewers described limited ability to revise delayed-symptom input, revert a progressed workout, or flex a weekly plan ([OBS-000173](../../data/observations.jsonl), [OBS-000175](../../data/observations.jsonl)).
- A TrainAsONE user manually adjusted duration because its injury-return progression seemed unsuitable ([OBS-000168](../../data/observations.jsonl)). This is one app-specific report.

**Adequacy evidence:** paid coach feedback helped one athlete rebuild consistently after PT ([OBS-000164](../../data/observations.jsonl)); staged clinician-led progression and daily practice worked for another ([OBS-000167](../../data/observations.jsonl)); PhysiApp’s program/reminders/logging were reported helpful ([OBS-000174](../../data/observations.jsonl)); Recover Athletics and Hinge Health reviews describe useful structured support ([OBS-000172](../../data/observations.jsonl), [OBS-000176](../../data/observations.jsonl)); and paid services received positive self-selected reviews ([OBS-000178](../../data/observations.jsonl), [OBS-000179](../../data/observations.jsonl)).

## Safety and scope boundary

The 20 persisted records concern tracking, progression, plan handoff, exercise adherence, scheduling, and clinician/coach communication. No record is used to diagnose an injury, select treatment, provide medical clearance, prescribe exercises, or replace a clinician. Acute/emergency, diagnosis-first, medication, and individualized-treatment content encountered during source review was excluded from the production dataset. Some records involve surgical or clinically managed histories; they are retained only when the attributable evidence concerns non-diagnostic post-care coordination or training workflow.

## Negative evidence and limitations

- Several accounts show a good PT program, coaching relationship, insurance-backed service, app, spreadsheet, or routine can adequately manage the transition.
- Observed failures may be individual clinician quality, app configuration, cost/access, athlete adherence, or medical-context variability rather than a repeatable product gap.
- The corpus is 50% Reddit, app reviews are self-selected, and forum/review-site accounts may not represent typical recreational athletes.
- Direct payer, insurer role, post-adoption frequency, economic consequence, and willingness to pay for a separate coordination job remain unknown.
