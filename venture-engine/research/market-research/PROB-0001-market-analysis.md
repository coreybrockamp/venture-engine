# PROB-0001 Market Analysis - Healthcare Information and Task Coordination

## Executive Summary

**ASSESSMENT:** This is a recurring, commercially relevant care-management job with both free substitutes and paid adjacent services. Evidence supports carrying the problem forward for review, not assuming a new product is needed.

## Problem Definition

Adult children need an accurate shared view of appointments, medications, records, and provider follow-ups.

## Existing Customer Evidence

**FACT:** Eleven linked observations span ten URLs and include workbooks, remote/local task division, medication and appointment work, and one request for an information-sharing app. See [Step 6 triangulation](../daily/2026-09-07-per-001-triangulation-checkpoint.md).

## Existing Solution Categories

Patient portals; shared spreadsheets and password managers; medication apps; family communication tools; care-coordination apps; and human care concierge services.

## Competitor Landscape

- CareBridger: shared tasks, calendar, notes, and care reports; published early-access plans from CA$0 to CA$18.99/month.
- Medisafe: medication-specific mobile app; App Store lists Premium options including US$4.99/month and US$39.99/year.
- CaringBridge: no-cost private health updates and support coordination.
- Wellthy: human coordinator; US$475/month private access or US$400/month for three months.

## Current Alternatives

**FACT:** Google Sheets, LastPass, portals, calendars, and family messages are already used. They work when a family maintains them; they create manual upkeep and do not automatically resolve authorization or fragmented-provider boundaries.

## Existing Spend

**FACT:** Paid medication subscriptions and private care-concierge plans demonstrate category spend. They do not establish willingness to pay for a new coordination product.

## Pricing Evidence

See [Medisafe App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/medisafe-medication-management/id573916946), [CareBridger pricing](https://carebridger.com/pricing), and [Wellthy plans](https://www.wellthy.com/plans-details/).

## Customer Complaints

**FACT:** One public Medisafe discussion cited subscription cost and manual entry friction. This is isolated feedback, not a recurring validated complaint pattern.

## Search / Demand Signals

**FACT:** Step 6 recorded solution-oriented query patterns and an explicit public-Q&A app request. Query volume is unavailable.

## Market Structure

**ASSESSMENT: HIGH fragmentation.** Free communication tools, point medication apps, generic software, and high-touch services solve different pieces.

## Market Size

**FACT:** AARP/NAC reports 63 million U.S. family caregivers, 59 million caring for adults. **UNKNOWN:** the share with this job and paid-software intent.

## Why Current Solutions Work

They are cheap or free, familiar, and can be adequate for a small stable care team.

## Why Current Solutions Fail

**FACT:** Observations show information split across tools and people. **UNKNOWN:** whether this reflects a durable product gap rather than insufficient setup or authorization constraints.

## Evidence For Commercial Importance

Published paid substitutes, recurring care tasks, and an explicit request for shared information tooling.

## Evidence Against Commercial Importance

Free tools were reported helpful; sensitive health information, portals, and switching friction may favor existing tools or services.

## Key Unknowns

Who pays; what workflow is abandoned; willingness to switch; and whether the buyer needs software, a human service, or both.

## Market Analyst Assessment

Existing spend: MODERATE. Active demand: MODERATE. Current solution satisfaction: MEDIUM. Market fragmentation: HIGH. Reachability: MEDIUM. Recurring need: HIGH. Commercial importance: HIGH. Confidence: 74.
