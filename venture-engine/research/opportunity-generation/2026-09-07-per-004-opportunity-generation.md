## Residual-Pain Assessment

### PROB-0008 — Scope Control

**Classification: C — PROCESS-DEPENDENT.**

**FACT:** The records show scope loss and recurring renegotiation (OBS-000067, OBS-000070), but they also identify the normal controls: explicit scope, revision limits, deposits, revised estimates, written approval, and contracts (OBS-000068, OBS-000086, OBS-000087). Bonsai, HoneyBook, Dubsado, and PandaDoc provide supporting proposal, agreement, approval, and workflow capabilities (COMP-0012 through COMP-0015).

The residual question—whether an established consultant reliably misses scope drift despite disciplined process and pays separately for economics or scope review—remains **UNKNOWN**. A generic scope-creep product, change-order tool, AI SOW generator, advisory offer, or productized review service would therefore rest on an unsupported willingness-to-pay assumption.

### PROB-0009 — Client-to-Cash Handoffs

**Classification: D — ALREADY SOLVED for a general product; residual exception work is UNKNOWN.**

**FACT:** Isolated payment, calendar, and data-transfer friction is documented (OBS-000076 through OBS-000080). However, a veteran freelancer reported roughly ten minutes per month for invoicing with a template/script (OBS-000074), and paid-suite users report consolidated workflows (OBS-000075, OBS-000079, OBS-000082). The four reviewed substitutes cover the core client-to-cash sequence at published entry prices (COMP-0012 through COMP-0015).

No evidence identifies a repeatable reconciliation or exception decision that survives competent setup, has a measured economic cost, and earns separate payment. Another CRM, client portal, invoicing tool, automation layer, integration, setup service, or optimization advisory is not promoted.

## Buyer, Pricing, and Economic Outcome

**FACT:** The likely user, buyer, and beneficiary would often be the solo consultant, and the possible outcomes are less uncompensated work, better effective rate, faster payment, or lower administration. Buyer clarity does not demonstrate demand.

**UNKNOWN:** separate willingness to pay, price, purchase trigger, and incremental economic benefit for either residual job. Existing subscription prices validate category spending only; they do not justify an add-on price or a service fee.

## Differentiation Test

Both candidate areas fail the required differentiation test. Better process discipline, templates/scripts, coaching, or the scope/document and client-operations functions in Bonsai, HoneyBook, Dubsado, and PandaDoc are plausible, cheap current alternatives. The evidence does not show a sufficiently distinct outcome that would cause an established consultant to pay separately.

## Rejected Opportunity Directions

- **Generic scope-creep or change-order software:** rejected; established tools enable the documented controls, while the remaining failure is primarily boundary-setting and enforcement.
- **AI SOW/proposal/contract generator:** rejected; no evidence that document creation, rather than agreement discipline, is the residual job.
- **Scope or pricing advisory/productized review:** rejected; no target-persona payment evidence or repeatable post-process failure was found.
- **Another consultant CRM, all-in-one suite, client portal, or generic invoicing product:** rejected; incumbent coverage is broad and some users report adequate workflows.
- **Generic workflow automation or integration layer:** rejected; isolated defects do not identify a persistent, separately purchasable exception workflow after competent configuration.
- **Client-operations setup/optimization service:** rejected; implementation demand and payment beyond vendor onboarding/self-service are not evidenced for this persona.

## What Must Be Established Before Reconsideration

1. **PROB-0008:** recurring, income-confirmed evidence that competent process and existing tools still fail to surface or monetize scope drift, plus evidence of payment for help.
2. **PROB-0009:** a specific recurring reconciliation or exception task after competent suite configuration, its time/cash consequence, and evidence that consultants pay beyond current tools or lightweight workflows.
