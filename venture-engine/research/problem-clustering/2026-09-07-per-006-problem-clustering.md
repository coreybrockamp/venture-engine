# PER-006 Problem Clustering — Owner of an Anxious or Reactive Dog

It created no market analysis, competitor record, Opportunity, score, experiment, offer, landing page, or automation. “Support” and “progress” describe owner information and service-access needs only; no clinical, diagnostic, medication, or treatment-efficacy claim is made.

## PROB-0010 — Training-Progress Information Continuity

**Problem statement:** Owners managing a reactive dog can struggle to record, compare, and communicate training incidents, trigger context, and change over time, leaving them uncertain whether to maintain, adapt, or discuss the current non-clinical management plan.

**JTBD:** When reactions vary during a long training process, help me retain and share a low-friction history so I can recognize patterns and have informed conversations with the people supporting my dog.

**Evidence:** OBS-000088, OBS-000091, OBS-000092, OBS-000102, and OBS-000110. One record reports weekly paid support with unclear progress; four document tracking or existing-solution experience. The root is owner information continuity, not an assumption that software is required.

**Commercial signals:** existing spend **WEAK** for this specific job; active solution seeking **MODERATE**; failed prior solutions **WEAK**; recurring management burden **STRONG**; lifestyle/economic consequence **WEAK**; current solution adequacy **MEDIUM**; buyer clarity **HIGH**.

**Type:** **TRACKING / INFORMATION-LED.** The available evidence supports repeated memory, comparison, and handoff friction, not a diagnosis or a claim that tracking improves outcomes.

**Disconfirmation:** A trainer-provided spreadsheet reportedly helped an owner (OBS-000092), and a dedicated app is already useful to some users despite requested features (OBS-000110). Standalone willingness to pay is unknown.

## PROB-0011 — Navigating Professional Behavior-Support Access and Fit

**Problem statement:** Owners seeking help for a reactive dog can face repeated uncertainty choosing accessible, affordable, and appropriate professional or structured support when prices, availability, provider fit, and perceived value vary, risking delayed, repeated, or unsatisfying spending decisions.

**JTBD:** When I need more help than self-directed training, help me identify and access an appropriate source of support for my situation so I can make a financially informed next-step decision without relying only on fragmented recommendations.

**Evidence:** OBS-000089, OBS-000090, OBS-000093, OBS-000096, OBS-000103, OBS-000105, OBS-000107, and OBS-000108. The set includes a paid low-value consultation, substantial quotes, a paid course described as unhelpful, specialist-affordability tradeoffs, a full class, and local/trust uncertainty.

**Commercial signals:** existing spend **MODERATE**; active solution seeking **STRONG**; failed prior solutions **MODERATE**; recurring management burden **MEDIUM**; lifestyle/economic consequence **MODERATE**; current solution adequacy **MEDIUM**; buyer clarity **HIGH**.

**Type:** **ACCESS-LED.** The owner is navigating availability, affordability, perceived value, and fit among existing services and structured programs. This does not assess provider quality, clinical suitability, or treatment efficacy.

**Disconfirmation:** A US$1,200 private package was reported helpful (OBS-000097); low-cost self-guided material was reported helpful (OBS-000104); and existing classes/courses can report progress (OBS-000106, OBS-000112). A local service may be more appropriate than any scalable intervention.

## Rejected, Merged, and Reframed Themes

- **Failed training attempts / plateaus:** merged into PROB-0011 as a contributor to support-selection and value uncertainty. Three records do not establish that training broadly fails, and outcome causes are unknown.
- **Trainer-selection trust:** merged into PROB-0011 because trust, local fit, cost, and availability all form one owner-level access decision.
- **Training continuity / owner implementation:** not promoted separately. The three records include working existing courses/classes and overlap with progress information and professional-support decisions.
- **Travel and specialized-care constraints:** not promoted. Four records show concrete consequences and some specialty care spend, but all are Reddit, the trigger is episodic, and severe-case concentration is **HIGH**. It remains a research theme, not a well-validated Problem.
- **Daily management / lifestyle adaptation:** not promoted. The evidence is consequential but sparse and partly demonstrates adaptation rather than a distinct unmet owner job.
- **Care coordination:** rejected. The corpus mentions multiple people in individual tracking discussions but lacks repeated evidence that fragmented histories or conflicting plans create a distinct owner burden.

## Severe-Case Bias

Both created Problems are **MEDIUM** severe-case-bias hypotheses. The data comes from self-selected support communities and course/app reviews, so treatment failures and difficult cases are likely overrepresented. PROB-0011 may extend beyond severe cases because price, class availability, and provider-fit choices can occur at several severity levels, but the data does not measure typicality. PROB-0010 may be most relevant to owners in prolonged, highly engaged training processes rather than every reactive-dog owner.

## Strongest Problems by Current Evidence

1. **PROB-0011:** strongest for active search, independent support, direct spending/quotes, and financially consequential next-step decisions. It remains a service/access hypothesis, not an identified product gap.
2. **PROB-0010:** strongest for recurring owner information burden across multiple tools and manual workarounds. Existing spreadsheets and apps materially weaken any assumption of an unmet standalone-tool need.

## Evidence Gaps for a Future Market Analyst

- Typical cost, wait time, geography, and repeat spend for qualified professional support.
- Whether owners pay for help selecting, accessing, or coordinating support beyond the service itself.
- Whether progress tracking improves owner decision quality or is adequately handled by spreadsheets, trainers, or existing apps.
- Frequency and generalizability of travel/boarding constraints outside severe cases.
- Clear owner willingness to pay beyond professional services, courses, and apps.
