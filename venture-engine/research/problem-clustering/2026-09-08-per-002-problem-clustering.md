## PROB-0015 — Rehab-to-training workflow coordination

**Problem statement:** Recreational athletes returning from a prolonged or recurring injury can struggle to carry already-given clinical constraints, coaching guidance, and between-session response information into a coherent non-clinical training routine, especially when guidance conflicts or a training tool cannot reflect a needed adjustment.

**JTBD:** When I am returning to sport under already-defined care or coaching guidance, help me keep agreed constraints, plan changes, and useful between-session updates coordinated in my training routine so I do not manually reconcile incompatible instructions or lose relevant context.

**Trigger and desired outcome:** A transition from formal care to training, conflicting clinician/coach input, a documented plan change, or a need to record an already-defined response after activity should yield a coherent routine without requiring the athlete to reconcile providers or tools alone.

**Evidence chain:** OBS-000160, OBS-000162, OBS-000165, OBS-000168, OBS-000170, OBS-000171, OBS-000173, and OBS-000175. The clearest operational post-adoption case is OBS-000160: a runner with coach and physio support received incompatible plans and had to reconcile them after the professionals would not coordinate. OBS-000168 reports one adaptive-training-app adjustment; OBS-000173 and OBS-000175 report narrow post-adoption Exakt workflow friction. OBS-000162 and OBS-000170 describe missing structured transition support, but do not establish competent-care failure. OBS-000165 and OBS-000171 show intent for structured progression or coaching, not a separate buyer.

**Type:** **WORKFLOW-LED early hypothesis.** It is about preserving and reconciling already-given information in training execution. It is not a claim that the system should determine injury status, interpret symptoms clinically, select a treatment, clear activity, prescribe exercises, or replace clinician/coach judgment.

**Commercial signal:** Existing spend is **category evidence only**. The corpus contains PT, coaching, plans, apps, insurance-backed programs, and recovery services, but does not identify a buyer or payer for a separate coordination layer, or willingness to pay after competent alternatives.

**Disconfirmation:** Paid coach feedback helped one athlete rebuild consistently after PT (OBS-000164); a staged clinician-led progression and daily commitment worked for another (OBS-000167); remote post-PT trainer support worked (OBS-000169); Recover Athletics, PhysiApp, and Hinge Health reviews describe useful structured support (OBS-000172, OBS-000174, OBS-000176); and paid services received positive reviews (OBS-000178, OBS-000179). These records materially weaken any inference that a distinct residual paid job already exists.

## Themes not promoted

- **Return-to-activity progression management:** not promoted separately. It overlaps materially with PROB-0015 but frequently requires clinical or coaching judgment. Several accounts also show clinician-led programs, coaching, or routine adherence working adequately (OBS-000161, OBS-000164, OBS-000167, OBS-000169).
- **Symptom/load tracking and delayed-response interpretation:** not promoted separately. OBS-000163 involves self-managed symptoms after foregoing PT and risks clinical interpretation; OBS-000173 and OBS-000175 are narrow product-specific recording/progression friction. The records do not establish a recurring, tool-independent operational job.
- **Rehab adherence and continuity after PT:** not promoted. The evidence is primarily adherence, program delivery, or service adequacy: nightly routine (OBS-000161), staged physio program (OBS-000167), Recover Athletics (OBS-000172), PhysiApp (OBS-000174), and Hinge Health (OBS-000176). It does not justify a separate workflow Problem.
- **Training-app and rehab-plan handoff:** not promoted separately. OBS-000168 is one TrainAsONE report, while OBS-000173 and OBS-000175 concern the same Exakt product across regional listings. The evidence is product-specific and is retained only as a possible manifestation within PROB-0015.
- **Rehab-tool access and subscription stack:** not promoted. OBS-000177 documents a reported extra subscription cost while awaiting care, not an independently supported coordination job; it is also clinically adjacent.
- **Provider quality, diagnosis, treatment, clearance, and general training judgment:** not promoted. These are outside the persona and safety boundary, or cannot be separated from clinician/coach judgment on this evidence.

## Evidence-quality constraints for any later review

- Post-adoption operational failure is limited: OBS-000160 is the clearest cross-provider coordination case. App friction is narrow and product-specific; missing structure after care does not demonstrate competent-care failure.
- Supporting evidence comes from eight URLs and three source types, but Reddit supplies half of it. App reviews and community posts are self-selected; the two Exakt listings are not independent vendor-level confirmation.
- The supporting set has no Tier 1 payment evidence. Paid service and app use elsewhere in the corpus establish category spend only, not a distinct buyer, payer, price, or willingness to pay.
- Recurrence, prevalence, time cost, economic consequence, and persistence after competent clinicians, coaches, apps, and process remain unknown. Any Market Analysis must treat these as constraints, not evidence of a commercial residual.
