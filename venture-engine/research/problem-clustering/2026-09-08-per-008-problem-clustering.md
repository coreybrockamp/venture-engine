## PROB-0014 — One-on-one commitment and follow-through

**Problem statement:** New people managers can struggle to maintain reliable follow-through on recurring one-on-one commitments, development actions, and performance context across direct reports, relying on memory and a mix of notes, calendars, templates, spreadsheets, or management tools.

**JTBD:** When I am managing recurring one-on-ones and follow-up commitments for my direct reports, help me retain the relevant context and reliably carry actions forward so I do not overlook commitments or reconstruct prior discussions.

**Trigger and desired outcome:** A recurring one-on-one, new development/performance commitment, missed meeting, or need to prepare follow-up should result in a usable record of commitments, priorities, notes, and next actions.

**Evidence chain:** OBS-000138, OBS-000141, OBS-000143, OBS-000147, OBS-000150, OBS-000155, OBS-000156, and OBS-000159. The clearest direct failure is OBS-000138: a manager with four reports let a development commitment lapse for about a month. The remaining records establish recurring 1:1 and follow-up work, manual note/calendar friction, or existing-management-tool use/adequacy. They do not establish market-wide failure.

**Type:** **WORKFLOW-LED early hypothesis.** It is distinct from general management anxiety because it has recurring touchpoints and retained-action/context work. It is not a claim that the task requires software.

**Commercial signal:** Existing tool use is **WEAK category evidence only**; payer, price, buyer, and willingness to pay for a separate follow-through job are unknown. No commercial conclusion is made.

**Disconfirmation:** Shared documents/templates and action tracking work for some managers (OBS-000141); ordinary periodic 1:1 cadence can be adequate (OBS-000143); 15Five is reported useful for newer managers (OBS-000155); and a spreadsheet worked for performance clarity (OBS-000159). The 15Five carry-forward complaint (OBS-000156) is a single product-specific friction report with low-to-medium persona fit.

## Evidence-quality constraints for any later review

- The sample has adequate source-type diversity but remains concentrated in online manager communities: Reddit is 59% of all Scout observations; Hacker News is technology-adjacent; app/course reviews are self-selected.
- Direct first-time-manager fit is uneven outside the Reddit subset. The record distinguishes high, medium, and low fit instead of assuming it.
- Existing spend is category-level only. The evidence does not establish whether the manager, HR/L&D, a people leader, or the employer selects or pays for tools/training, nor willingness to pay separately for follow-through.
- Recurrence is qualitatively supported around 1:1 cycles, but prevalence, time cost, business impact, and persistence after competent use of current tools/processes remain unknown.
