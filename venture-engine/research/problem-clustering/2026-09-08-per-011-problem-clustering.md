**FACT:** This pass reviewed all 20 PER-011 Scout observations and created **zero Problems**. No market-analysis memo, competitor, Opportunity, score, experiment, offer, landing page, or automation was created.

## Structural-vs-implementation assessment

**FACT:** Mapping and exception work recur in some named paid stacks, especially where systems, custom fields, or transformations change. **UNKNOWN:** whether this persists after competent implementation in a repeatable subset of the narrow persona.

**ASSESSMENT:** The available evidence is primarily implementation/configuration-, reporting-architecture-, and bespoke-complexity-driven. It does not support a structural residual workflow separate from those causes. The current corpus must not convert custom mapping maintenance or a specific reporting gap into a general Problem.

## Ordinary-control and professional-judgment boundary

Financial reconciliation and review can be intentionally necessary for auditability, reporting assurance, and treatment of unusual transactions. OBS-000187, OBS-000191, and OBS-000199 are retained as evidence that ERP reconciliation, defined revenue rules, and manual journal adjustments are not automatically waste. Cases requiring contract/accounting interpretation or case-specific reporting logic were not promoted as operational Problems.

## Source, actor, and commercial-signal assessment

**FACT:** The Scout corpus contains 11 independent URLs across Reddit practitioner communities, review sites, and Hacker News. Supporting evidence for the most persona-relevant contract-change mechanism is only Reddit, so it has a **SOURCE DIVERSITY GAP**. Internal operators appear in some records, but implementation practitioners, consultants, and unclear-role participants materially contribute to the corpus.

**UNKNOWN:** frequency, labor cost, company/contract complexity, buyer authority, payer, procurement path, and post-competent-adoption persistence. Existing CRM, billing, ERP, reconciliation, and reporting spend does not establish a separate willingness to pay.

## CFD-originated mechanism check (non-canonical traceability)

**Predictive early signals:** The CFD premise correctly identified paid multi-system stacks, manual overrides, mapping/exception work, and reporting reconciliation as discoverable evidence.

**Misleading or insufficient signals:** The Scout did not show that these behaviors converge on one repeatable residual workflow after competent adoption. It instead revealed multiple explanations—implementation debt, custom architecture, legitimate control, judgment, and incumbent adequacy. The CFD-originated mechanism therefore did **not** survive as a formal Problem and narrowed materially to an unproven future evidence question.
