# PER-012 Problem Clustering — Construction Change-Order Financial Continuity

## Scope and result

**FACT:** This pass reviewed all 20 PER-012 Scout observations and created **zero Problems**. No market-analysis memo, competitor, Opportunity, score, experiment, offer, landing page, or automation was created.

The evidence confirms that paid construction project/accounting stacks can leave manual synchronization, correction, re-entry, spreadsheet reconciliation, and financial-workflow friction. It does not establish a recurring, structural change-order financial-continuity workflow that persists independently of Procore-specific behavior, connector/implementation conditions, contract-specific rules, and ordinary financial control. The evidence therefore does not meet the formal Problem-promotion bar.

## Structural-vs-product-specific assessment

**FACT:** Several independent accounts report manual work after adoption of Procore plus Sage or another accounting system. **FACT:** the corpus also includes a Procore/Viewpoint case and Autodesk/Vista discussion, but the former lacks a connector and the latter is consultant-led and tax-rule specific.

**ASSESSMENT:** The available evidence does not support a structural recurring Problem independent of Procore-specific implementation behavior. Cross-company recurrence is meaningful only within a Procore-centered ecosystem, and cross-platform recurrence does not survive the operator-evidence test. The clusterer must not turn a vendor/product pattern into a persona-wide Problem.

## Ordinary-control and professional-judgment boundary

**FACT:** A spreadsheet may serve as a cross-check of accounting data, and monthly budget/forecast review, approval-state review, and verification can be intentional financial controls (OBS-000202, OBS-000207, OBS-000209–000210). These activities are not automatically waste or unmet demand.

**FACT:** Contract-specific markup/deduct rules and the interpretation of project or change-order status influence some of the reported reconciliation work (OBS-000207, OBS-000210). Those facts may require contract, forecasting, or accounting judgment rather than a repeatable operational product workflow. They were not promoted.

## Source, actor, and commercial-signal assessment

**FACT:** The Scout corpus contains 12 independent URLs across Reddit practitioner communities, review sites, and one industry community. The core synchronization-correction evidence has six URLs and two source types, but its detailed firsthand examples are concentrated in Reddit and Procore. The non-Procore evidence has two URLs and two source types but no internal-operator account.

**FACT:** One paid-integrator figure, repeated manual sync, multi-person correction work, and spreadsheet/re-entry behavior are preserved. **UNKNOWN:** recurrence after competent adoption, prevalence across contractor types or stack maturity, frequency, labor cost, billing/WIP consequence, buyer authority, payer, procurement path, and separate willingness to pay.

## CFD-originated mechanism check (non-canonical traceability)

**Predictive early signals:** CFD correctly surfaced paid construction systems, integration spend, manual synchronization, correction work, and spreadsheet tie-outs that independently appeared in Scout.

**Misleading or insufficient signals:** Cross-company recurrence did not become a structural cross-platform mechanism. The strongest cases shared Procore, and the non-Procore signals were consultant/implementation-specific. The same corpus showed that configuration, supported integrations, consolidated alternatives, standardized administration, and appropriate controls can absorb much of the work. The CFD mechanism therefore did **not** survive clustering as a formal Problem.
