## Reliable source types

1. Public community/forum discussions: Reddit, AgingCare, ALZConnected, AARP, and Caring-for-Aging-Parents.
2. Public Q&A: Caring-for-Aging-Parents; AgingCare question pages.
3. Search intent: partial, useful for query discovery and explicit solution-seeking signals when paired with attributable pages.

## Recommended next Scout sources

1. AgingCare forum — strong public caregiver narratives, dated URLs, care and spend context.
2. ALZConnected — public caregiver burden and administration discussion; use with diagnosis-scope caution.
3. AARP Caregiving Connect — public caregiver discussion, broader demographic context.
4. Caring-for-Aging-Parents Q&A — public visitor questions; verify moderation/date per item.
5. Reddit — retain as a comparison source, but cap concentration and do not count multiple subreddits as types.

## Access limitations and implications

Quora is blocked. App-review text, Google Play reviews, and YouTube comments remain partial because reliable review/comment extraction was not established. Resource organizations may offer authoritative context but not first-person evidence. A future Scout run can meet the three-type requirement through forum/community, public Q&A, and search-intent evidence; however, it should explicitly label search-intent evidence and avoid treating it as purchase behavior.
